Before you order

  1. Confirm the variant. Match the seller’s size chart to a garment or pair of shoes you already own. Note the item ID, colour, and size for each candidate.
  2. Review warehouse QC photos if available. Check the label, stitching, and colour. For shoes, ask for an insole measurement if the size chart is unclear. For bags, check the straps and closures.
  3. Compare the delivered cost. Add the selected item price, domestic delivery, any agent fees, international shipping, and applicable taxes or duties. Check whether your shipping route bills by actual or volumetric weight.

Frequently asked questions

Where do the product links go?

Each product opens its HipoBuyFind listing. If you plan to use OopBuy as your shopping agent, look for the original seller URL on the listing and confirm that OopBuy accepts it before ordering.

What if the listing shows several prices?

Select the exact size and colour first. Use the price displayed for that variant to compare options and estimate your total.
